GENERALS HYPERSONIC — RELEASE NOTES / KNOWN ISSUES

EA has not endorsed and does not support this product.

Free unofficial fan mod for Command & Conquer: Generals – Zero Hour.
Iran joins as a fourth faction, with strike drones and ballistic missiles,
in skirmish against the AI and in multiplayer. Packages are listed on the
website with their version and checksums. The Android build is experimental.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
v1.0.0-alpha.2 (November 2026)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
NEW
- Qadr cluster missile: its warhead splits into 10 submunitions in the final
  dive, covering a wide area.
- Gaza recon drone: stays in the air, detects stealth, drops bombs and fires
  Almas missiles.
- Bavar-373: a long-range SAM truck that deploys on its own, replacing the Herz-9.
- Persian unit voices and a new English announcer, all recorded by volunteers.

CHANGED
- Khorramshahr-4 reworked: submunitions released one by one, and a silo with
  blast doors.
- Faster, cheaper missile and drone strikes, and a bigger Drone Facility.
- Interception now depends on the enemy faction, with Countermeasures upgrades
  to get past it.
- Regular missile launches show the launch site to enemies for a few seconds and
  alert them, so a missile base can be targeted.
- New missile and drone sounds, and a bigger missile explosion.
- Smoother drone flight, burning wrecks, cook-offs, and revenge drones from
  destroyed buildings.
- New building art: construction stages, visible upgrades and damaged looks.
- Wider camera zoom-out, in Options and with Ctrl + mouse wheel.
- Custom skirmish maps from your Zero Hour maps folder, with the Iran AI.
- Clearer tooltips, and shortcuts that no longer clash with the game's own keys.

FIXED
- A mid-game freeze caused by the sound system. If the game still freezes for
  you, please send the Logs folder from Documents\Generals Hypersonic Data.
- An Easy Iran AI that could stop building early in a match.
- Android: updated, with crash and display fixes.

KNOWN LIMITS
- Phones and PCs cannot play together in this version.
- Saved games and replays from alpha.1 do not work in this version, and every
  player in an online or LAN game needs the same version.
- Performance of big salvos on weak phones has not been measured.
- The files are unsigned, so Windows may show a security warning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
v1.0.0-alpha.1 (October 2026): first public alpha
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Formats: Windows installer, portable ZIP and an experimental Android APK.
Campaign missions, Generals Challenge and Iran sub-generals are not included.

EXPECTATIONS
- Bugs, unfinished or placeholder art/icons, evolving balance and possible crashes.
- Iran's skirmish AI is still being tuned.
- Multiplayer requires the same Hypersonic build and executable for every player.
- Phones and PCs cannot play together.
- Testing on more PCs and across game installation variants is still in progress.
  No finished compatibility claim is made.

The mod uses a separate mod folder and Documents\Generals Hypersonic Data;
the original Zero Hour folder is not changed by the installation.
Your own legal Zero Hour 1.04 is required; no retail game archives are included.

Report actual/expected behavior, map, factions, AI difficulty, mod version,
replay and ReleaseCrashInfo.txt after a crash. Use bug-report-template.txt.
Matching engine source accompanies every distributed engine release.
