The fourth faction / Iran

Generals HypersonicGenerals Hypersonic

A free fan mod for Command & Conquer: Generals – Zero Hour. Iran joins as a fourth faction, with strike drones and ballistic missiles.

Drones and missiles. No fighter jets.

In Hypersonic, Iran strikes from range with ballistic missiles and drones, and defends its airspace with surface-to-air missiles.

Launch

Prepare. Launch. Reload.

Stock your facilities with drones and missiles, then launch salvos at targets across the map.

  • Drone Facility
  • Ballistic Missile Facility
Missiles

Hard to stop.

Ballistic missiles from the Fateh-110 to the hypersonic Fattah. Enemy air defenses can intercept them, so speed and salvos matter.

  • Fateh‑110
  • Kheibar Shekan
  • Fattah

Superweapon Khorramshahr: one missile, ten warheads.

Drones

Launch and let them fly.

Pick a point on the map and the strike drones head there on their own. Cheap and expendable, so send several.

  • Shahed‑238
  • Arash

Close the sky.

This alpha is available by invitation only. Invited testers receive access instructions.

There is no public download or tester sign-up form on this site yet. The public download will be posted here when it is ready.

Skirmish vs AI and multiplayer. The Android build is experimental.

What you need

  • A Windows PC, or a 64-bit Android 9+ phone (experimental).
  • Your own copy of Zero Hour 1.04 (not included). Don't have it? Zero Hour is part of Command & Conquer: The Ultimate Collection on Steam and the EA app.
  • The mod installs in its own folder with its own user data; your Zero Hour game folder is not changed.
Installation for invited testers
  1. Have Zero Hour 1.04 installed.
  2. Run the installer, or extract the portable ZIP into its own folder outside Zero Hour.
  3. Launch the Generals Hypersonic shortcut, or HypersonicZH.exe from the portable folder.

What is in the alpha?

This alpha includes skirmish against the AI and multiplayer. Expect bugs, unfinished art and balance changes. Multiplayer requires everyone to use the same mod and engine build. Campaign missions and Generals Challenge are not included.

Portable ZIP and file checksums

Extract the ZIP into its own folder, outside your Zero Hour folder, and run HypersonicZH.exe. If the game is not found, follow the portable setup instructions.

Checksums will accompany the release packages.

Steam copies, screen resolution and custom maps

Steam: Steam copies of Zero Hour work. The installer finds Zero Hour in your Steam library, also on other drives. If it does not, select the folder that contains game.dat and INIZH.big.

Screen resolution: in the game open Options, pick your screen size under Resolution (for example 1920×1080) and choose Accept. Or, with the game closed, edit Documents\Generals Hypersonic Data\Options.ini and set a line like Resolution = 1280 720. If the game runs slowly, use a smaller resolution and set Detail to Low.

Camera: Next update The camera zooms out much further than in Zero Hour (height 550 instead of 310), so you can follow missiles and drones across the map.

Custom maps: Next update Skirmish maps in your normal Zero Hour maps folder (Documents\Command and Conquer Generals Zero Hour Data\Maps) appear in the map list automatically, and the Iran AI plays on them too.

Updating to a new version

Installer: run the new setup. It installs over the old version and removes files the new version no longer uses; there is no need to uninstall first. Close the game before you start.

Portable ZIP: delete the old mod folder (never your Zero Hour folder) and extract the new ZIP in its place.

Saved games and replays from an older version do not work in a new version, and online or LAN games need the same version for every player. Your options are kept.

How do I report a bug?

Include your mod version, the steps to reproduce the problem, what happened and what you expected. Add the map, factions and AI difficulty, if they apply. Attach a replay if you have one and, after a crash, ReleaseCrashInfo.txt from Documents\Generals Hypersonic Data.

Casting call

Voice Iran’s units

Volunteer voice acting for the mod: 15 unit roles in Persian and one Announcer in English. A phone in a quiet room is enough, and every volunteer is credited in the game and on this site.

فراخوان صداگذاری

صدای یونیت‌های ایران شوید

صداگذاری داوطلبانه برای ماد: ۱۵ نقش یونیت به فارسی و یک نقش گوینده به انگلیسی. یک گوشی در اتاقی ساکت کافی است و نام همه‌ی داوطلبان در تیتراژ بازی و روی همین سایت می‌آید.

  1. 01

    Choose a role

    Read its lines on Telegram. They are suggestions: change them or improvise.

    یک نقش انتخاب کنید

    جمله‌هایش را در تلگرام بخوانید. جمله‌ها فقط پیشنهادند؛ می‌توانید تغییرشان دهید.

  2. 02

    Message @mswi_2004

    Say which role you want and wait for the reply that it is yours.

    به @mswi_2004 پیام بدهید

    بگویید کدام نقش را می‌خواهید و منتظر تأیید بمانید.

  3. 03

    Record on your phone

    Once the role is yours: in a quiet room, hold the phone about 20 cm from your mouth and say each line 2–3 times. Telegram voice messages are fine.

    با گوشی ضبط کنید

    پس از تأیید نقش، در اتاقی ساکت گوشی را حدود ۲۰ سانتی‌متر از دهانتان نگه دارید و هر جمله را ۲ تا ۳ بار بگویید. پیام صوتی تلگرام هم قبول است.
