Version 1.0.0-alpha.2

What’s new

Two new weapons, Persian voices, new building art and reworked strikes.

New missile

Qadr cluster missile

A ballistic missile whose warhead splits into 10 submunitions in its final dive, covering a wide area.

New drone

Gaza recon drone

An armed drone that stays in the air, detects stealth, drops bombs and fires Almas missiles.

Strikes and defense

  • Bavar‑373: a long-range SAM truck that deploys on its own, replacing the Herz‑9.
  • Khorramshahr‑4 reworked: submunitions released one by one, and a silo with blast doors.
  • Faster, cheaper missile and drone strikes, and a bigger Drone Facility.
  • Interception now depends on the enemy faction, with Countermeasures upgrades to get past it.
  • Missile launches now show the launch site to enemies for a few seconds and alert them.

Look and sound

  • Persian unit voices and a new English announcer. Meet the cast ↓
  • New missile and drone sounds, and a bigger missile explosion.
  • New building art: construction stages, visible upgrades and damaged looks.
  • Smoother drone flight, burning wrecks, cook-offs, and revenge drones from destroyed buildings.

Playing the game

  • Wider camera zoom-out, in Options and with Ctrl + mouse wheel.
  • Custom skirmish maps from your Zero Hour maps folder, with the Iran AI.
  • Clearer tooltips, and shortcuts that no longer clash with the game’s own keys.
  • Fixed a mid-game freeze caused by the sound system.
  • Fixed the Easy Iran AI, which could stop building early in a match.
  • Android: updated, with crash and display fixes.

Public alphav1.0.0-alpha.2Windows PC

v1.0.0-alpha.2 is a public alpha for Windows, with an experimental Android build. Expect bugs and unfinished art.

Includes skirmish against the AI and multiplayer.

What you need

  • A Windows PC, or a 64-bit Android 9+ phone (experimental).
  • Your own copy of Zero Hour 1.04 (not included). Don't have it? Zero Hour is part of Command & Conquer: The Ultimate Collection on Steam and the EA app.
  • The mod installs in its own folder with its own user data; your Zero Hour game folder is not changed.
Install in three steps
  1. Have Zero Hour 1.04 installed.
  2. Run the installer, or extract the portable ZIP into its own folder outside Zero Hour.
  3. Launch the Generals Hypersonic shortcut, or HypersonicZH.exe from the portable folder.

What is in the alpha?

This alpha includes skirmish against the AI and multiplayer. Expect bugs, unfinished art and balance changes. Multiplayer requires everyone to use the same mod and engine build. Phones and PCs cannot play together in this version. Campaign missions and Generals Challenge are not included.

Portable ZIP and file checksums

Extract the ZIP into its own folder, outside your Zero Hour folder, and run HypersonicZH.exe. If the game is not found, follow the portable setup instructions.

Checksums will accompany the release packages.

Steam copies, screen resolution and custom maps

Steam: Steam copies of Zero Hour work. The installer finds Zero Hour in your Steam library, also on other drives. If it does not, select the folder that contains INIZH.big.

Screen resolution: in the game open Options, pick your screen size under Resolution (for example 1920×1080) and choose Accept. Or, with the game closed, edit Documents\Generals Hypersonic Data\Options.ini and set a line like Resolution = 1280 720. If the game runs slowly, use a smaller resolution and set Detail to Low.

Camera: The camera zooms out much further than in Zero Hour, so you can follow missiles and drones across the map. Pick the distance under Camera Zoom-Out in Options, or hold Ctrl and use the mouse wheel.

Custom maps: Skirmish maps in your normal Zero Hour maps folder (Documents\Command and Conquer Generals Zero Hour Data\Maps) are read automatically, and the Iran AI plays on them too.

Updating to a new version

Installer: run the new setup. It installs over the old version and removes files the new version no longer uses; there is no need to uninstall first. Close the game before you start.

Portable ZIP: delete the old mod folder (never your Zero Hour folder) and extract the new ZIP in its place.

Saved games and replays from an older version do not work in a new version, and online or LAN games need the same version for every player. Your options are kept.

How do I report a bug?

Include your mod version, the steps to reproduce the problem, what happened and what you expected. Add the map, factions and AI difficulty, if they apply. Attach a replay if you have one and, after a crash or freeze, ReleaseCrashInfo.txt and the Logs folder from Documents\Generals Hypersonic Data.

Drones and missiles. No fighter jets.

In Hypersonic, Iran strikes from range with ballistic missiles and drones, and defends its airspace with surface-to-air missiles.

Launch

Prepare. Launch. Reload.

Stock your facilities with drones and missiles, then launch salvos at targets across the map.

  • Drone Facility
  • Ballistic Missile Facility
Missiles

Hard to stop.

Ballistic missiles from the Fateh-110 to the hypersonic Fattah. Enemy air defenses can intercept them, so speed and salvos matter.

  • Fateh‑110
  • Kheibar Shekan
  • Qadr
  • Fattah

Superweapon Khorramshahr‑4: one missile, 14 submunitions and a main warhead.

Drones

Launch and let them fly.

Pick a point on the map and the strike drones head there on their own. Cheap and expendable, so send several.

  • Shahed‑136
  • Shahed‑238
  • Arash

Recon Gaza: an armed, reusable drone that stays in the air under your command.

Ground forces

Armor behind the strikes.

Tanks and rocket artillery finish what the missiles and drones start.

  • Karrar Tank
  • Zolfaghar Heavy Tank
  • Fajr‑5 MLRS
Air defense

Close the sky.

SAM sites and mobile launchers shoot down aircraft and missiles. Anti-tank bunkers stop armored attacks.

  • Khordad‑15 SAM Site
  • Bavar‑373 Mobile SAM
  • Dehlavieh ATGM Bunker
